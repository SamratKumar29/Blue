# Leave and Core Working Hours Policy

## Purpose
This policy sets expectations for working schedules, availability, and time off to ensure high-performance collaboration while respecting personal boundaries and sustainable wellbeing.

## Core Working Hours
Acme operates with core collaboration hours between **09:30 AM and 06:00 PM** local time (with standard regional allowances). Team members are expected to be reachable via team communication channels and available for scheduled syncs during these hours.

## Quiet Hours and Deep Focus
To encourage deep engineering focus and prevent fatigue:
- No non-emergency communications or automated nudges should be dispatched before 09:00 AM or after 06:30 PM.
- Wednesday afternoons are designated as meeting-free deep work blocks across all engineering pods.

## Time-Off & Leave Notice
1. **Planned Leave:** Planned annual vacation or personal days must be logged in the HR portal at least 48 hours in advance for single-day absences and at least two weeks in advance for absences spanning three days or longer.
2. **Sick Leave:** Please notify your pod lead on Slack or via email by 09:30 AM on the day of absence. No documentation is required for up to two consecutive sick days.
3. **Emergency Off-Hours:** Team members are empowered to prioritize family and health emergencies without prior approval.
